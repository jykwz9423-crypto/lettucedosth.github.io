Where Sand Becomes a Palace

 It reflects my growth through the process of making. Each work begins with fragments—uncertain ideas, loose impressions, and quiet emotions.
Like sand, they are unstable and difficult to hold.

Through exploration and repetition, these fragments gradually take form.
Whether in illustrations, designs, or constructed spaces, my practice is about learning how to shape uncertainty into something intentional and meaningful.
